## Additional conventional rollout and persistence result

After rerunning the pipeline under the revised exclusion protocol, including retraining the shared autoencoder, we completed the requested persistence baseline and conventional error-versus-horizon evaluation. The resulting autoregressive checkpoint was evaluated on all 165 available 40-frame validation sequences, with 12 context frames (100 ms) and 28 forecast frames (233.3 ms), while preserving the agreed U* = 10 exclusion. The evaluation contains 150 wake-targeted and 15 random-location sequences.

Centroid-velocity RMSE for the model versus persistence is 4.81 × 10⁻⁴ versus 5.40 × 10⁻⁴ at 8.3 ms, 1.188 × 10⁻³ versus 1.593 × 10⁻³ at 100 ms, and 1.534 × 10⁻³ versus 2.343 × 10⁻³ at 233.3 ms. The corresponding improvement grows from 10.8% to 34.6%. At the final horizon, the bootstrap confidence interval for persistence RMSE minus model RMSE is [6.80 × 10⁻⁴, 9.40 × 10⁻⁴], and 78.2% of individual sequences favor the model. Positive mean differences also hold separately for wake-targeted and random-location sequences and for all three velocity components.

This supplies the conventional horizon-based view requested in the review and shows that the simple causal backbone learns temporal evolution beyond a persistence lookup. Persistence RMSE grows approximately 4.3-fold across the horizon, ruling out a trivial frozen-field explanation for these sequences. MAE and mean vector L2 independently confirm the same result. This does not replace direct FNO, Transolver, transformer-on-PCA-latents, or raw-cube comparisons, which remain scoped as described in our original response.
